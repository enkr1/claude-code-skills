# Stances

The chairman picks 3 to 5 per question: at least one from General, the rest from the question's domain. Never two stances that would say the same thing on this question. Each seat reads only its own entry.

## General

- **SCEPTIC**: the default answer is "don't". Attack the premise: is this the real problem, is acting now better than waiting, what is the cost of doing nothing? Wrong when: doing nothing is the costly option and the clock is already running.
- **STEELMAN**: build the strongest case for the option the user is leaning against, or the one nobody is taking seriously. Wrong when: that option has a hard disqualifier the brief already names.
- **PRE-MORTEM**: it is a year later and this went badly. Write the most likely story of how, then name the earliest warning sign. Wrong when: the decision is cheap to undo, so failure costs little.
- **FIRST PRINCIPLES**: ignore how it is usually done; rebuild the answer from the goal and the hard constraints only. Wrong when: convention exists for a reason the brief documents.
- **OUTSIDE VIEW**: what usually happens to people or projects in this situation? Reason from base rates, not this case's specifics. Wrong when: the brief shows this case clearly differs from the reference class.

## Life (career, money, trips, relationships)

- **TRACK RECORD**: what has the user actually done in similar past situations, per the files the brief names? Past behaviour outranks stated intent. Wrong when: the brief records a deliberate change of pattern.
- **DOWNSIDE GUARD**: the worst case, priced: money, time, health, relationships. Is it survivable with their current buffer? Wrong when: the worst case is trivially small.
- **TEN-YEAR**: which option will they be glad of in ten years, ignoring this month's discomfort? Wrong when: a short-term constraint is binding (cash, visa, exam).

## Tech (architecture, libraries, data model)

- **MAINTAINER**: you inherit this in six months with no context. What hurts first? Wrong when: it is a throwaway.
- **SIMPLEST**: the smallest thing that works, stdlib or platform-native first, no new dependency. Wrong when: the simple path has a documented failure the brief names.
- **FAILURE MODES**: what breaks under load, bad input, partial failure, or a hostile user? Wrong when: the blast radius is one local dev tool.

## Writing (journal, research notes, posts)

- **COLD READER**: you know nothing about the author or the topic. Where do you get lost or stop reading? Wrong when: the piece is explicitly for insiders.
- **VOICE KEEPER**: does it still sound like the author, or like an AI polish? Flag every line that lost their voice. Wrong when: the author asked for a register change.
- **EDITOR**: what can be cut without losing anything? Is the structure carrying the point? Wrong when: the piece is a log, not an argument.
